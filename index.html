Founder & Managing Director Celeste
Building businesses that solve real problems

From hospitality to Sri Lanka's quick-commerce frontier

Janik Jayasuriya is known for vision, resilience, and innovation in Sri Lanka's business landscape. As the Founder and Managing Director of Celeste, he introduced the country's first and fastest-growing on-demand cloud supermarket, changing how customers access groceries and everyday essentials.

A graduate of the Glion Institute of Higher Education in Switzerland with a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration in Hospitality Management, Janik began his career in hospitality. When COVID-19 disrupted movement and supply chains, he identified the need for dependable on-demand grocery delivery and moved quickly to bring the quick-commerce model to Sri Lanka.

"For him, business is not just about profits; it is about impact."

His leadership has supported job creation, local producers, small businesses, and communities. Beyond the boardroom, his background in competitive sport shapes a leadership style grounded in discipline, teamwork, calculated risk-taking, resilience, and excellence.

Principles

Core Values

The principles that guide every decision, every delivery, and every relationship at Celeste.

Serve People First

Rooted in Janik's hospitality background - leadership is fundamentally about serving people. Every team member and every customer deserves genuine care and respect.

Innovation

Pioneering quick-commerce in Sri Lanka means constantly challenging the status quo. Technology and creativity drive every solution we build for our customers.

Integrity

Do the right thing, always - even when no one is watching. Build trust through transparency, accountability, and ethical decision-making at every level.

Customer Obsession

Every decision begins and ends with the customer. From farm to doorstep, we obsess over reliability, freshness, and convenience at every touchpoint.

Resilience

Built during the world's most challenging crisis, we embody the spirit of turning obstacles into opportunities. Every setback is a lesson that fuels our growth.

Teamwork

Success is never an individual achievement. Like rugby, every person on the field matters. We build each other up, share wins, and grow together as one team.
